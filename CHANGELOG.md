# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (3 November 2026): first Community Edition

- The organic knowledge atlas, search, notebook, code graphs and exports from Adamanteum, as a standalone local-first app.
- A door for AI: any MCP client can search and read your vault with a revocable, read-only token.
- Bring your own AI: any OpenAI-compatible provider, local or hosted, with automatic fallback between connections you add.
- 18 fictional sample notes open on first run. No account, key or cloud service needed.
- Private connectors, company hand-offs and bundled backends are not part of this edition.
- Known gaps: see "Not yet" in the README.
